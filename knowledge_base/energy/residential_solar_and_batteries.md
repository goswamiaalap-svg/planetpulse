# Residential Rooftop Solar and Battery Energy Storage

Generating on-site electricity with residential photovoltaic (PV) solar panels directly offsets emissions from grid power, providing one of the largest single-step carbon reductions for homeowners.

## Emissions Metrics & Carbon Payback

1. **Grid Displacement Factor**: In grid regions dominated by fossil fuels (average 0.80 kg CO2/kWh), a standard 5 kW to 8 kW residential solar system generates between 6,000 and 11,000 kWh annually, offsetting 4,800 to 8,800 kg of CO2 per year.
2. **Emissions Payback Time**: Silicon solar modules require embedded manufacturing energy, but repay their lifecycle carbon footprint within 1.5 to 2.5 years of operation. Over a 25-to-30-year operational lifespan, rooftop solar produces net clean power with lifecycle emissions under 0.04 kg CO2e/kWh (95% lower than grid thermal power).
3. **Battery Storage Integration**: Lithium-iron-phosphate (LFP) home batteries (e.g. 10–13 kWh capacity) store midday surplus solar for evening peak consumption, avoiding the use of fossil-fired "peaker plants" during high-emissions evening grid hours.

## Actionable Recommendations

- **Self-Consumption Optimization**: Schedule high-draw appliances (washing machines, dishwashers, EV chargers) during peak daytime solar generation hours.
- **Community Solar Subscriptions**: For renters and apartment dwellers unable to install physical panels, subscribing to a certified community solar project achieves equivalent grid decarbonization benefits.
