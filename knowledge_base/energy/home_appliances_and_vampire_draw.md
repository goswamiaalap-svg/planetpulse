# Home Appliances, Induction Cooking, and Vampire Power

Household plug loads and major appliances account for approximately 25% to 30% of residential electricity consumption. Strategic upgrades and phantom load mitigation deliver measurable weekly carbon reductions.

## Energy Draw Benchmarks

1. **Vampire / Standby Power**: Devices on continuous standby (game consoles, smart TVs, desktop computers, Wi-Fi routers, microwave clocks) consume between 5% and 10% of total household residential electricity—equivalent to 200–400 kWh (160–320 kg CO2) per home annually.
2. **Induction vs. Gas Cooking**: Electric induction cooktops transfer 85% to 90% of electromagnetic energy directly into cookware, compared to only 35% to 40% efficiency for residential gas burners. Transitioning to induction eliminates indoor nitrogen dioxide (NO2) pollutants and reduces cooking emissions by 40% on standard electrical grids.
3. **Refrigeration & Laundry Efficiency**: ENERGY STAR certified refrigerators and heat-pump clothes dryers consume 40% to 50% less energy than units manufactured before 2015. Washing clothes in cold water (30°C / 86°F) cuts washing cycle electricity use by up to 90%.

## Practical Mitigation Steps

- **Smart Power Strips**: Connect entertainment and home office systems to master-controlled or occupancy-sensing power strips that automatically sever phantom loads.
- **Eco-Mode Utilization**: Run dishwashers and clothes washers on eco-cycle mode with full loads during off-peak hours.
