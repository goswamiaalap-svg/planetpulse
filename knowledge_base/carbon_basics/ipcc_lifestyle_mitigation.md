# IPCC Demand-Side Mitigation and the "Avoid-Shift-Improve" Framework

The IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6) confirms that demand-side interventions, behavioral shifts, and efficient service provision can reduce global greenhouse gas emissions across end-use sectors by **40% to 70% by 2050**.

## The Avoid-Shift-Improve (ASI) Paradigm

The IPCC categorizes personal and collective decarbonization actions into three complementary tiers:

1. **Avoid (Reduce Demand)**: Eliminating unnecessary carbon-intensive consumption without sacrificing quality of life:
   - Teleworking and digital conferencing to avoid long-distance business travel.
   - Reducing residential food waste and optimizing appliance standby draws.
   - Trip-chaining errands to avoid duplicate driving distance.
2. **Shift (Modal & Dietary Transition)**: Switching from high-carbon modes and foods to lower-carbon alternatives:
   - Shifting from single-occupancy driving to public transit, cycling, and walking.
   - Transitioning from high-emission animal diets (beef, lamb) to plant-forward vegetarian meals.
   - Choosing passenger rail over short-haul regional flights.
3. **Improve (Technological Efficiency)**: Upgrading necessary equipment to highest-efficiency technologies:
   - Replacing gas or oil furnaces with high-COP air-source heat pumps.
   - Transitioning from internal combustion vehicles to electric vehicles (EVs).
   - Installing rooftop solar PV and smart LED illumination.

## Individual Agency & Systemic Decarbonization

Sustained tracking and feedback mechanisms (such as PlanetPulse's weekly budgets) enable users to identify their top emitting sectors and apply the highest-leverage ASI strategy for consistent weekly reductions.
