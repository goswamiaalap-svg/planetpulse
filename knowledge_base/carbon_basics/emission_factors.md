# Carbon Footprint Basics and Standard Emission Factors

A personal carbon footprint measures the total greenhouse gas emissions caused directly and indirectly by an individual's lifestyle, transportation, dietary consumption, and domestic utility usage, expressed in kilograms of carbon dioxide equivalent (kg CO2e).

## Deterministic Emission Factors Used in PlanetPulse

To ensure reproducible and scientifically verifiable auditing, PlanetPulse employs fixed standard benchmark factors:

- **Car Travel**: 0.20 kg CO2 per kilometer (typical single-occupant gasoline vehicle).
- **Bus Travel**: 0.08 kg CO2 per passenger-kilometer (average municipal public transit bus).
- **Flight**: 0.25 kg CO2 per passenger-kilometer (commercial domestic/short-haul passenger aircraft).
- **Electricity**: 0.80 kg CO2 per kilowatt-hour (average coal/gas-dominant grid power).
- **Vegetarian Meal**: 0.50 kg CO2 per standard plant-forward serving.
- **Non-Vegetarian Meal**: 2.00 kg CO2 per standard red meat/poultry serving (4x higher than vegetarian).

## Target Pacing and Reduction Principles

- Rather than aiming for immediate zero emissions, behavioral science demonstrates that setting achievable weekly reduction targets (e.g. 20–30 kg CO2/week) and tracking progress with positive, non-shaming feedback produces sustained long-term lifestyle decarbonization.
- The highest-leverage initial adjustments are consistently modal shifts away from single-occupancy driving and substituting animal protein with nutrient-dense plant foods.
