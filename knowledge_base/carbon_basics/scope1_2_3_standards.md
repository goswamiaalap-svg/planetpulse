# Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 Carbon Accounting Standards

The Greenhouse Gas Protocol provides the global standard framework for measuring, categorizing, and managing greenhouse gas emissions across all economic sectors and personal carbon accounting.

## The Three Emission Scopes Defined

1. **Scope 1 (Direct Emissions)**: Greenhouse gases released directly from sources owned or controlled by the individual or organization:
   - Tailpipe combustion emissions from driving a personal gasoline or diesel vehicle (0.20 kg CO2/km).
   - On-site natural gas or heating oil combustion in residential boilers, water heaters, and gas stoves.
2. **Scope 2 (Indirect Utility Emissions)**: Emissions associated with the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, or cooling consumed by the end-user:
   - Grid electricity consumed for residential lighting, cooling, and appliances (average 0.80 kg CO2/kWh on fossil-dominant grids).
   - Electricity drawn to charge a Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV).
3. **Scope 3 (Value Chain & Embodied Emissions)**: All other indirect emissions that occur across the upstream and downstream lifecycle of consumed products and services:
   - Commercial aviation and public bus passenger transit.
   - Agricultural and land-use emissions embodied in food purchases (0.50 kg CO2 per vegetarian meal vs. 2.00 kg CO2 per non-vegetarian meal).
   - Embodied manufacturing emissions in consumer electronics, clothing, and vehicles.

## Application in Personal Carbon Auditing

In PlanetPulse, individual activities are dynamically mapped to their deterministic emission factors, providing clear visibility across direct fuel combustion (Scope 1), home power usage (Scope 2), and purchased transit and dietary consumption (Scope 3).
