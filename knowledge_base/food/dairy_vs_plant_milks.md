# Dairy Milk vs. Plant-Based Milk Alternatives

The production of dairy milk generates substantially higher greenhouse gas emissions, land use, and water consumption than plant-based alternative beverages (oat, soy, almond, and pea milk).

## Lifecycle Emissions and Resource Footprint per Liter

1. **Cow's Dairy Milk**: Emits approximately **3.0 to 3.2 kg CO2e per liter**, primarily driven by enteric fermentation (methane from ruminant digestion), manure management, and feed crop cultivation.
2. **Oat Milk**: Emits **0.90 kg CO2e per liter** (70% reduction vs dairy) with low water requirements and minimal land use.
3. **Soy Milk**: Emits **0.98 kg CO2e per liter** (68% reduction vs dairy) and provides equivalent protein density with nitrogen-fixing agricultural benefits.
4. **Almond Milk**: Emits **0.70 kg CO2e per liter** (78% reduction vs dairy), though has higher freshwater irrigation demands in arid climates.
5. **Rice Milk**: Emits **1.20 kg CO2e per liter** (60% reduction vs dairy; higher than other plant milks due to paddy methane).

## Practical Dietary Swaps

- **Morning Coffee & Cereal Swap**: Replacing 250 ml of dairy milk daily with oat or soy milk saves ~0.55 kg CO2e per day, accumulating over 200 kg CO2e in annual savings.
- **Plant-Based Yogurts and Cheeses**: Transitioning to coconut, cashew, or oat-based fermented dairy alternatives eliminates the outsized methane footprint of dairy herds.
