# Food Waste Prevention and Residential Composting

Globally, approximately one-third of all food produced for human consumption is lost or wasted, accounting for 8% to 10% of total global greenhouse gas emissions. Reducing domestic food waste is ranked by Project Drawdown among the top 3 most effective global climate solutions.

## Emissions Mechanics & Landfill Methane

1. **Embedded Agricultural Emissions**: When food is thrown into the garbage, all embedded emissions from land clearing, fertilizer synthesis, irrigation pumping, harvesting, and cold-chain refrigerated transport are completely wasted.
2. **Anaerobic Landfill Degradation**: Organic waste buried in municipal landfills decomposes anaerobically (without oxygen), generating methane (CH4)—a greenhouse gas with a global warming potential 28 to 36 times greater than CO2 over a 100-year timescale.
3. **Household Impact**: The average individual discards between 70 and 120 kg of edible food annually, generating ~250 to 400 kg of unnecessary CO2e per person.

## High-Impact Household Interventions

- **Meal Planning and FIFO Storage**: Practice "First-In, First-Out" pantry and refrigerator storage to consume perishables before expiration.
- **Aerobic Composting & Bokashi**: Composting food scraps aerobically (with oxygen) converts organic matter into nutrient-rich soil humus, sequestering carbon and preventing anaerobic methane emissions entirely.
