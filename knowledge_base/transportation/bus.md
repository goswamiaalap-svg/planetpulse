# Public Transit & Bus Travel Climate Benefits

Using public transportation such as urban buses and rapid transit systems is one of the most effective daily actions individuals can take to reduce urban congestion and carbon footprints.

## Climate Advantages of Buses

1. **Emission Intensity**: Average diesel and hybrid urban buses produce approximately 0.08 kg of CO2 per passenger-kilometer when carrying typical passenger loads, compared to 0.20 kg CO2 per passenger-kilometer for a single-occupant passenger car.
2. **Space and Congestion Savings**: A single fully loaded municipal bus can take up to 40 private cars off the road, cutting secondary emissions from traffic congestion and idling.
3. **Electrification**: Modern zero-emission battery electric buses (BEBs) powered by renewable municipal grids produce up to 85% fewer lifecycle emissions than internal combustion vehicles.
4. **Active Transport Linkage**: Public transit trips inherently incorporate walking or cycling to and from transit hubs, fostering a healthier lifestyle with minimal environmental impact.
