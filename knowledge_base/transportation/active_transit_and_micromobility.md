# Active Transit, Bicycling, and Electric Micro-Mobility

Over 50% of all personal vehicle trips globally are under 5 kilometers (3 miles)—a distance ideally suited for active mobility (walking and conventional cycling) or electric micro-mobility (e-bikes and e-scooters).

## Emissions Comparison for Short Trips

1. **Walking & Conventional Bicycles**: Net operational emissions are effectively 0.00 kg CO2/km (only baseline human metabolic food consumption).
2. **Electric Bicycles (E-Bikes)**: E-bikes consume between 0.010 and 0.015 kWh per kilometer. Even on a fossil-heavy grid (0.80 kg CO2/kWh), an e-bike emits only 0.008 to 0.012 kg CO2 per kilometer—94% lower than driving a private gasoline car (0.20 kg CO2/km).
3. **Trip Substitution Impact**: Replacing one 10 km daily car commute with an e-bike saves 2.0 kg of CO2 daily (over 500 kg CO2 annually per commuter).

## Behavioral Strategies for High-Leverage Adoption

- **15-Minute City Trip Chaining**: Group local grocery runs, package pick-ups, and short errands into a single pedestrian or bike loop.
- **Intermodal Transit Integration**: Pair folding e-bikes or shared scooters with municipal bus and commuter rail systems for the "first-and-last mile" of longer commutes.
