# Aviation and Flight Emissions Impact

Commercial aviation has an outsized carbon impact per passenger-kilometer compared to ground transportation modes. Typical passenger aviation averages approximately 0.25 kg CO2 per passenger-kilometer for economy domestic and short-haul flights.

## Climate Implications of Air Travel

1. **High Altitude Forcing**: Aircraft engines release emissions directly into the upper troposphere, creating contrails and cirrus cloud formations that amplify radiative warming beyond carbon emissions alone.
2. **Short-Haul vs. Long-Haul**: Takeoff and climb-out phases account for a disproportionate percentage of total flight fuel burn. Flights under 500 km are 30% to 50% more carbon-intensive per kilometer than medium-haul flights.
3. **High-Speed Rail Alternatives**: Wherever electrified high-speed rail exists (e.g. routes under 800 km), choosing electric trains reduces passenger emissions by up to 90% compared to equivalent short flights.
4. **Virtual Collaboration**: Replacing routine business meetings with high-definition video conferencing eliminates long-distance travel emissions completely.
